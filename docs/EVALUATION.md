# Evaluate image analysis

Local HTTP/browser fixtures validate integration behavior, not Gemini's PPE accuracy. Live inference needs your free-tier API key.

Gather representative photographs covering activity, body visibility, equipment, lighting, occlusion and multi-person scenes. Include welding helmets versus hardhats, boots versus certified safety footwear, gloves, respirators, eye/ear protection, harnesses and clothing. Have a person label worn items, carried objects and uncertain observations separately.

Compare reported inventories and checks against these labels. Count missed visible items, invented items, incorrect person associations and confident claims about unverifiable equipment. Check cropped body parts remain uncertain, and empty scenes do not produce people or compliance conclusions. Measure latency and failure rate separately.

Record provider model and promptVersion. Recheck images after either changes. Analyze again retains the earlier inspection for comparison. Respect free quota and avoid automatic repeated reruns.

Human review is required. Photographs cannot establish hidden equipment, certification, fit, effectiveness or occupational compliance. The app does not display invented accuracy percentages.

## Machinery guidance

Include machinery-only scenes, people using machines, multiple machines, uncertain machine types and ordinary scenes without machinery. Have a competent reviewer label machine identity, visible hazards, typical/task-dependent risks and required versus unsuitable PPE. Compare against the manufacturer manual and a task-specific assessment, not image appearance alone.

Verify precautions cover relevant before-use, operation, energy isolation/maintenance and emergency response. Check rotating machinery never gets unconditional glove/loose-wearable recommendations; face shields supplement primary eye protection; exposure-related PPE remains conditional; unseen guards/interlocks are not confidently declared absent or functional. Check activity context changes appropriate guidance without overriding safety rules, and no procedures or specifications are invented.

A live workshop-lathe photo smoke test verified recognition, structured guidance, persistence and desktop/mobile rendering. It is one integration example, not evidence of comprehensive hazard coverage or reliable safety recommendations across machine types.
